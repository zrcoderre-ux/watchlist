# Watchlist

A phone-first PWA for keeping track of movies and shows you want to watch.

- **Discover** — swipe through trending, top-rated or new movies and shows.
  Right adds to your watchlist, left passes, up means you've seen it (and asks
  how it was). Filter by movies or TV, genre, and what's on your services.
  **For you** asks Claude for picks based on everything you've liked, wanted
  and passed on.
- **Watchlist** — a list or a swipeable stack of cards, filtered by type,
  genre and your services, sorted however you like. Each title shows where
  it's streaming, renting and buying in your country.
- **Tonight** — say what you're in the mood for and Claude picks the best few
  from your watchlist, or let it surprise you.
- **Add from anywhere** — search, or hand Claude a photo of a poster, a
  screenshot of a list (including your Google watchlist), pasted text, or a
  file, then tick which titles to add.
- **Your services** — pick the streaming services you have; availability comes
  from JustWatch via TMDB and refreshes every few days.
- Syncs between your devices, works offline, light and dark themes.

Setup: see [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).

Movie and TV data from [TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org/). This product uses
the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Streaming availability
from [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/).
